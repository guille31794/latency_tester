Safety instructions

  • Do not expose the device to liquids or temperatures above 40 °C.
  • Do not point the LED at eyes.
  • Use only the specified battery and charger (5 V / 3 A).
  • Do not open the case while connected to power.
  • In case of excessive heat or burning smell, disconnect immediately.

Device description

The device emits a light pulse (LED) toward the camera of the system under test. A photosensor detects when the monitor reproduces that stimulus. The time difference is the system's latency.

Components

  • Touchscreen (front): 7-inch capacitive. Only means of interaction.
  • Stimulus LED (side): turns on automatically during measurements.
  • Photosensor (opposite side): must face the monitor.
  • USB-C port (bottom): charging and power supply.

Power on / off

  • Power on: hold the power button for 2 seconds.
  • Power off: hold the ← button on the home screen for 3 seconds.

Do not disconnect power abruptly to avoid corrupting saved measurements.

Home screen

The main menu has four options:

  • Start measuring: opens the measurement screen.
  • Measure record: browse previous measurements.
  • Settings: configure language, font, and display modes.
  • Help: shows this manual and general information.

All screens have a yellow ← button to go back.

Taking measurements

1. Calibration (required before measuring)

  1. Point the photosensor toward the monitor area where the stimulus will appear.
  2. Press Calibrate.
  3. The LED will blink during the process (4 cycles). Do not move the device.
  4. Result: green = success, red = failure (reposition the sensor).

2. Configure parameters

  • Interval between measures: 100–2000 ms. Lower values = more data points.
  • Duration: 10–600 s. Total test duration.

3. Measure

  1. Point the LED toward the camera and the photosensor toward the monitor.
  2. Press Start.
  3. The button blinks green while measuring.
  4. When finished: green = success (graph shown), red = error.
  5. Results are saved automatically.

Press Stop at any time to cancel.

Reading the graph

  • Horizontal axis: elapsed time (s).
  • Vertical axis: measured latency (ms).
  • Each dot is an individual measurement.

Measurement history

Lists all stored measurements. Available actions:

  • Check: view graph and data (name, date, mean latency, interval).
  • Delete: permanently removes the selected measurement.
  • Rename: changes the measurement file name.

Settings

  • Language: Español, English, Polski.
  • Font size: 9–15 pt.
  • Colorblind mode: high-contrast color palette.
  • Night mode: dark theme for low-light environments.

Press Apply to save. Cancel to discard.

Both modes can be active simultaneously. Colorblind mode takes visual priority.
